A course can be completed while the work remains unchanged. Sometimes the employee has learned something useful but has no opportunity to apply it. Sometimes the requirement has already moved.

## Connect learning to the next requirement

I would connect development to the team’s next work change and return to it after the first assignment. What can the person now do? Where do they still need support? Learning teams need visibility of those changes early enough to contribute, rather than receive a late request to train everyone.

## Put the support into the plan

Agree one capability, protected practice time and a relevant assignment with a named reviewer. Use the feedback to adjust the next step. Revisit the requirement instead of treating the original training plan as fixed.

Without time, access and manager support, development becomes additional work the employee is expected to absorb. That is not a credible investment plan.
