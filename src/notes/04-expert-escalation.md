A routine query can become a serious issue very quickly. A missing payment may be a technical correction for payroll and an immediate financial concern for the employee. The service needs to recognise both.

## Keep the service responsive to the situation

I support self-service where it genuinely helps. But employees should not have to prove to a chatbot that their problem matters. Clear information, generalist support and specialist expertise need to work as one service, with a straightforward route between them and no requirement to repeat the whole story.

## Test the difficult route

Agree escalation triggers with the relevant specialists. Test fictional cases involving sensitive concerns and urgent payroll issues. Confirm who receives the case, what context transfers and when the employee should expect meaningful contact.

The route is only credible if the receiving team has capacity. Measure the time to useful help, not just the speed of transferring a ticket.
