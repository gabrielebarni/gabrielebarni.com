The onboarding checklist can be complete while a new starter remains unsure what to do first or whom to approach. From their perspective, the experience is one arrival, even if several functions deliver it.

## Look at the arrival from the employee’s side

HR can help connect the preparation without taking ownership of every task. The manager still needs to explain expectations, and colleagues need to make room for questions. AI may support a guide or routine information; it cannot create the working relationship on the manager’s behalf.

## Work backwards from a useful first assignment

Define an appropriate first contribution for the role. Work backwards through the access, knowledge and relationships it requires. After the first week, ask the new colleague which obstacles the planned process missed.

Do not turn speed into pressure to skip learning. Contribution should grow with clarity, support and the safeguards the role requires.
