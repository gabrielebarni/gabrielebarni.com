The demonstration works well. The important questions begin when the request is ambiguous, the policy is local or the model changes after implementation. That is the version of the product the organisation will have to manage.

## Evaluate the operating reality

I would bring HR, IT, security, privacy and procurement around one evaluation. We need shared answers on data handling, access, failure modes, support and exit. A generic assurance is not enough when the consequences sit across several functions.

## Use your difficult cases

Give the vendor synthetic cases covering your languages, exceptions and incomplete requests. Agree acceptance criteria and change-notification expectations before contracting. Make ownership of the ongoing checks explicit.

A benchmark or compliance claim does not validate the organisation’s particular use. Purpose, configuration, jurisdiction and day-to-day operation still need assessment.
