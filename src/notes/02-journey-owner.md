Imagine a colleague moving country. HR has issued the documents, payroll has opened a case and IT has received a request. Everyone has acted. The employee still cannot confirm the start date.

## Accountability across the handoffs

This is a coordination problem with a real business cost. Someone needs visibility of the whole move, authority to resolve conflicting priorities and accountability for the outcome. That person does not need to manage every specialist. They do need an agreed route to get decisions made when the process stops.

## Make the next step visible

Map one transfer from the first request to the point at which the employee can work. Agree the information required at each handoff, the receiving owner and when a delay must be escalated. Give the employee one reliable view of progress.

If the owner can only chase other teams, we have created another coordination role without solving the accountability gap.
