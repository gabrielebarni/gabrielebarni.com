Someone can write an excellent prompt and still put confidential information into the wrong tool or accept an answer that does not apply. That is a training gap with operational consequences.

## Define the capability through decisions

For HR, I would define AI literacy through a few practical decisions: when to use an approved tool, what information to protect, how to check a result and when to involve a specialist. Prompting belongs inside that practice. It should not become the main measure of capability.

## Practise with realistic situations

Use fictional situations from everyday work: an ambiguous policy question, a document summary and an unsupported recommendation. Ask colleagues what they would verify, what they would withhold and whether they would use AI at all.

Attendance is useful administration, not evidence of application. Check whether people can explain their decisions after the session.
