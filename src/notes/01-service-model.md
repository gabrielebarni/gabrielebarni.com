An HR transformation can spend months on the structure and still leave a manager asking the same question: who can help me get this done? That is a useful place to start the design discussion.

## What needs to be agreed

I would first agree three service commitments: straightforward requests are easy to complete, difficult cases reach the right expertise, and business leaders get support with organisational change. Then decide how shared services, HRBPs and specialist teams should deliver them. The structure matters. It just needs to answer a business question, rather than become the whole conversation.

## Where I would start

Choose one service, perhaps an internal transfer. Agree who owns it, what completion means and which handoffs are creating delays. Walk through a difficult case with the teams involved before discussing the platform.

Check where the effort goes. A saving in HR may simply mean more administration for line managers.
