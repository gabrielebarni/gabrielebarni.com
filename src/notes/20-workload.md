A team starts drafting faster with AI. Soon there is more material to check, more decisions to coordinate and an expectation that the team can take on additional volume. The original promise was to make work easier.

## Look at the whole workload

I would look at the redesigned day as well as the output. What has genuinely stopped? Which new demands have appeared? Can employees exercise more control, or are they simply processing more work? This is a work-design discussion, with wellbeing built into it.

## Agree what will change for the team

During a pilot, review interruptions, after-hours demands, perceived control and quality alongside speed. Agree how released capacity will be used. Use appropriate aggregated feedback, rather than monitoring individual behaviour unnecessarily.

Avoid speculative assessments of employees’ health or distress from their communications. Concerns need appropriate support and qualified advice.

<summary>Research behind this note</summary>

WHO recommends addressing workplace psychosocial risks through organisational interventions that target working conditions, with meaningful worker involvement. Individual support alone is not the whole response.
