A manager asks a simple question about leave. The answer changes depending on the country, the employee group and which version of the policy the assistant finds. The technology may respond perfectly to the wrong document.

## The work behind a reliable answer

This is why I would treat knowledge quality as part of service delivery, with proper ownership and ongoing capacity. HR needs to know which source is current, where local exceptions apply and who can approve a correction. Buying a better interface does not resolve contradictory guidance.

## Start with the questions people actually ask

Start with the twenty questions the service team receives most often. Agree a short approved answer for each, including applicability, the source policy, an owner and a review date. Make it easy for agents to flag an answer that no longer works.

More articles do not necessarily mean better support. Check whether the correct answer reaches every channel after a policy changes.
