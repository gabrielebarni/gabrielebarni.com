A manager has a difficult conversation ahead and is struggling to find a clear way to begin. There may be value in rehearsing a fictional version before meeting the person. The preparation needs boundaries.

## Prepare without pre-judging the person

I would use this to practise language, questions and listening, not to diagnose the employee’s motivation or predict their reaction. The manager still has to understand the context and remain open to what the colleague says. A generated script should never become a substitute for that attention.

## Evaluate the quality of the preparation

Test invented scenarios against an approved conversation framework. Ask HR reviewers whether the exercise encourages clarity, curiosity and sensible escalation. Have managers explain what they would change in their preparation.

Keep identifiable case material out of unapproved tools. Sensitive employee-relations matters need the relevant human expertise and policy checks.
