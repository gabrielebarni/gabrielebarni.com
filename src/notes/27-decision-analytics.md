A team has high attrition. It is tempting to request a predictive model immediately. I would first ask what action the leader can take, what the available data can tell us and what else may explain the pattern.

## Keep the analysis connected to action

That discussion can lead to a much more useful brief. Sometimes a descriptive analysis and conversations with employees are enough to identify the next step. Greater technical sophistication is valuable when it improves the decision, rather than simply making the output look more authoritative.

## Agree a short decision brief

Write down the question, owner, available actions, minimum data and alternative explanations. Use aggregated information where it is sufficient. Review whether the intervention helped, not just whether the model produced an interesting result.

Be particularly careful with individual labels. A statistical association does not establish an employee’s intention and can influence how their manager treats them.
