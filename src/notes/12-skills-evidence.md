A system may list hundreds of skills and still leave a leader unsure who could take on a critical assignment. The missing element is often evidence, rather than a more detailed taxonomy.

## Keep the profile connected to decisions

I would keep the practical question in view: who can deliver this work now, who could develop into it and what would give us confidence? Interest, proficiency and willingness to move are all useful, but they are different information. Combining them into one score can obscure the talent conversation.

## Use evidence people can discuss

Choose one job family and define a manageable set of capabilities through examples of work. Record the source and date of the evidence. Give employees a way to correct their profile and discuss development opportunities with their manager.

A course completion shows participation. A job title shows a position held. Neither, on its own, establishes the level of capability required for a new assignment.
