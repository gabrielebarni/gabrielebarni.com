A pilot can produce answers faster while creating more review work for specialists. If that effort sits in another budget, it is easy to miss. The business still pays for it.

## Assess the complete service

I would put the gains and the additional demands on the same page. Include ongoing knowledge maintenance, exceptions, employee experience and incidents, alongside delivery speed. The purpose is to reach an honest decision about value, including whether a promising idea needs redesign.

## Agree the decision criteria early

Agree a baseline and success criteria before the pilot. Include licence, integration, training, review and support costs. Define what evidence would justify continuing, changing scope or stopping.

Keep the criteria stable when results disappoint. A small pilot can produce useful learning without yet supporting a reliable financial return.
