An organisation can spend considerable time debating a chatbot while a scheduling system already shapes employees’ hours and opportunities. The second may have a much more direct effect on their working lives.

## Follow the decision, not the label

I would include allocation, monitoring and evaluation tools in the people-governance discussion. Who understands the rules? Who can explain an outcome? Who can correct it? These questions matter to employees regardless of the technology label.

## Start with decisions people experience

Inventory the systems that allocate or evaluate work. Name the accountable owner and correction route for each. Ask employees for examples they find difficult to understand, then examine those cases with the system owner.

A manager who can approve a recommendation but cannot challenge it has limited authority. Test the practical ability to intervene.
