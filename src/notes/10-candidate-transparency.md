Summarising a CV, transcribing an interview and helping rank applicants are different activities. A candidate deserves a more useful explanation than a statement that the organisation uses innovative technology.

## Make the process understandable

I would explain the purpose of each relevant tool, what information it uses and who can address a concern. This is also an operational test. If the recruiting team cannot describe the process clearly, it may not yet have enough clarity to manage it.

## Align the explanation with the workflow

Map the AI-supported steps with recruitment, privacy and employment specialists. Prepare short, accurate explanations and a workable contact route. Check the requirements in each jurisdiction before launch.

Only promise human review where the process actually provides it. Warm language cannot compensate for an inaccurate commitment.

<summary>Research behind this note</summary>

The UK ICO's 2024 recruitment-tool audits highlighted fair processing, data minimisation and clear explanations to candidates. These findings concern UK data protection, not a universal legal checklist.
