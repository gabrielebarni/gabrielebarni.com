A pilot reports time savings. The next question should be straightforward: what happened with that time? If nobody can answer, the business case is still incomplete.

## Agree the intended benefit

Released capacity may support better service, deeper analysis, learning or additional volume. It may also disappear into fragmented schedules and more messages. I would agree the intended use with the business owner before rollout, rather than assume that a tool will create the organisational change for us.

## Follow the capacity through

Choose one activity the team wants to do more of, or one backlog it needs to reduce. Track whether that happens. Include verification, training and corrections in the time estimate, and be honest about the range.

Time released, output increased and budget reduced are different outcomes. Multiplying estimated minutes by salary does not establish a cash saving.

<details>
<summary>Research behind this note</summary>

A six-month experiment across 66 firms found time savings among users given an integrated AI tool, but no detected shift in task quantity or composition from individual-level access. The NBER paper was revised in November 2025.

Source: [Dillon et al. · Shifting Work Patterns with Generative AI](https://www.nber.org/papers/w33795) (NBER, revised 2025)

</details>
