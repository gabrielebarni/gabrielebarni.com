A colleague is identified for a development assignment. The fit looks strong, the employee is interested and the project sponsor is ready. Then the current manager cannot release them. The opportunity exists in the system, but not in practice.

## Resolve the operating conditions

A talent marketplace needs agreements around capacity and business priorities. Who covers the current work? How much time can the assignment take? What does the manager gain from developing talent for another team? These are leadership choices that a matching engine cannot settle.

## Make a small set of opportunities real

Pilot a few short assignments with agreed time allocation, release conditions and a learning sponsor. Allow people to apply as well as be suggested by the system. Review who gets access and which groups keep missing out.

Past assignments reflect past access to opportunity. Use those records thoughtfully; they are not a complete assessment of somebody’s potential.
