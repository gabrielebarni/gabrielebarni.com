A long-term workforce number can look reassuring in a presentation. Its usefulness depends on the assumptions underneath: demand, technology, adoption, process change and the capacity to train or redeploy people.

## Make the assumptions discussable

Those factors will not move together. I would separate them and identify which decisions remain sensible across several scenarios. The value of the exercise is knowing what to do now and what evidence would change the plan, rather than defending a forecast after its assumptions have moved.

## Define the signals for a change of course

For one function, compare limited assistance, widespread assistance and controlled automation. Describe the skills, delivery constraints and review workload in each. Agree practical triggers for hiring, development or redeployment.

External forecasts provide context. They do not replace local work analysis or the appropriate discussions with affected employees.
