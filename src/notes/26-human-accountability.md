A workflow may contain an approval step and still leave nobody in a realistic position to challenge the recommendation. The reviewer is busy, the rationale is unclear and the process rewards fast acceptance.

## Make the decision-maker effective

For an important people decision, I would want a named decision-maker who can inspect the evidence, ask further questions and pause the process. They need the authority to disagree without being treated as an obstacle. Accountability has to work under normal operating pressure, not only in the design document.

## Test the right to challenge

Clarify who recommends, decides, pauses and responds to a challenge. Test a fictional case containing a plausible error. Observe whether the reviewer can identify it and get it resolved.

Some proposed uses should be narrowed or rejected. Adding an approval step does not automatically make them appropriate.
